# ETF rotation challenger — predeclaration

This protocol was committed before downloading or evaluating the dedicated
research snapshot. It tests a materially different long-only asset-allocation
hypothesis across liquid, unleveraged U.S.-listed stock, bond and gold ETFs.

The search is intentionally limited to 16 monthly configurations. Selection
uses only the 2018–2020 development and 2021–2023 validation partitions. The
chosen configuration must be written to a locked artifact before opening the
2024–30 October 2026 evaluation partition. No result from this experiment may
alter or reset the selected BTC/ETH forward-paper account.

The exact machine-readable rules are in `protocol.json`. Historical success
would nominate a separate prospective challenger; it would not prove future
profitability or authorize broker orders.
